1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE
================================================================================

ApniPehChaan is an autonomous personal AI agent that empowers individuals
to proactively discover where their sensitive personal information (PII) is exposed
online, cross-correlate multi-breach attack vectors, assess statutory rights
under privacy legislation, and execute enforceable Right-to-be-Forgotten legal
erasure notices and direct self-serve account closures.

The Core Mental Model: An Autonomous Multi-Agent Swarm, Not a Passive Scanner
  - A scanner executes a passive pipeline: input -> fixed scan -> table of dumps.
    The user is left with the entire cognitive and legal burden: "Which of these
    are mine? Which can legally be deleted? Who do I serve? Did they comply?"
  - An autonomous agent operates in a closed loop:
    GOAL -> DISCOVER -> DECIDE (LEGAL JUDGEMENT) -> ACT (HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP) -> VERIFY
    The agent coordinates 3 specialized sub-agents and 22 tools, calculates
    mathematical identity attribution, maps statutory rights under India DPDP Act
    2023 Section 12 or EU GDPR Art. 17, drafts formal legal notices with registered
    Grievance Officers, awaits user approval for dispatch, monitors compliance
    deadlines, and escalates to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) if
    deadlines lapse.

Six Core Architectural Guarantees:
  1. Zero False Positives / Zero Collision Risk:
     Attribution strictly relies on exact unique identifiers (Email, Indian Phone,
     PAN, Aadhaar, UPI ID, or declared handles). A person's legal name alone is
     NEVER used to claim a third-party account or breach.
  2. Operating Breached Fiduciary Rights (DPDP Act 2023 s.12):
     Operating commercial companies in breaches (IIMjobs, Zomato, Yatra, LinkedIn,
     Canva, etc.) are treated as accountable Data Fiduciaries. While dark web
     dumps cannot be un-published from hacker forums, data principals hold a
     statutory right to require the operating corporate fiduciary to permanently
     delete their records from active and backup servers.
  3. Dynamic Fiduciary Resolution for Unknown Companies (No Pre-Saved Cheating):
     Zero static pre-saved mock profiles or fake fallback emails (privacy@<slug>.com).
     The Legal Counsel Agent dynamically investigates arbitrary company names,
     identifying registered corporate entities, official DPO emails, and registered
     headquarters via Gemini / Groq.
  4. Cross-Exposure Threat Surface Intelligence:
     Correlates multi-breach data into actionable attack vectors: Credential Stuffing,
     Spear-Phishing, and SIM Swap vulnerabilities with prioritized mitigations.
  5. 100% Free & Keyless Operation:
     Zero dependency on paid API subscriptions (e.g. HIBP paid enterprise tier).
     Combines keyless feeds: XposedOrNot breach catalog, Hudson Rock Cavalier
     infostealer malware infections, k-anonymity SHA-1 password prefix checks,
     and 26 live profile namespace verifiers.
  6. Pure Real-World Data & Zero Synthetic Sandbox:
     All synthetic, fake, or simulated broker sandbox environments have been
     permanently eradicated. Every result reflects authentic real-world intelligence.

4. DYNAMIC DATA FIDUCIARY DISCOVERY ENGINE (ZERO PRE-SAVED CHEATING)
================================================================================

Why Static Dictionaries Were Removed:
  Earlier implementations relied on a pre-defined dictionary of ~15 companies.
  In practice, breaches occur at thousands of arbitrary startups and global services.
  Hardcoded lists give an impression of artificial pre-canned data.

The Dynamic Fiduciary Discovery Pipeline:
  1. When an unknown company (e.g. Zomato, Canva, Cred, Blinkit) is processed:
     `resolve_fiduciary(company_name, country="IN")` is invoked.
  2. The Legal Counsel Agent (Gemini with Groq backup) analyzes the company:
     - Detects registered corporate entity name (e.g. "Zomato Limited", "Canva Pty Ltd").
     - Resolves statutory Grievance Officer / DPO email.
     - Resolves registered corporate headquarters.
     - Maps applicable statute (DPDP s.12 for India, GDPR Art. 17 for EU, CCPA for US).
  3. Dark-Web Dump Disambiguation:
     If the source is an unattributed dark-web compilation (e.g. Naz.API, Collection #1),
     the agent identifies that no corporate fiduciary exists, flags `erasure_available=False`,
     and prescribes password rotation.

Two-Way Interactive Live Sync in Legal Studio:
  - Entering a company name in `#legal-chat-company-input` immediately synchronizes
    `#legal-company` and re-triggers `renderStatutoryNoticePreview()`.
  - Conversely, changing `#legal-company` updates the chatbot input.
  - The live notice preview dynamically updates recipient addresses, legal citations,
    and statutory penalty clauses on every keystroke.

   Every exposure, breach record, and profile hit comes from verified real feeds

3. Password Security & Masking:
   - Password input includes an eye toggle (`Show/Hide`) and defaults to dotted
     password masking (`••••••••`) to protect credentials during screen sharing.
   - Passwords are never sent in plaintext over external networks. Only the 5-character
     SHA-1 prefix is transmitted to the k-anonymity verification API.
   - Passwords and raw secret hashes are explicitly redacted from trace logs and
     database events.

4. Candidate Profile Isolation:
   Handles guessed or derived from email addresses are quarantined in the
   Unconfirmed Candidates panel. They are never added to the official risk score
   or removal plan until explicitly confirmed by the user.

11. ALGORITHMIC INNOVATIONS & ANTI-FABRICATION GUARANTEES
================================================================================

1. Aadhaar vs. Mobile Disambiguation (`backend/pii/recognizer.py`):
   Aadhaar uses the Verhoeff dihedral group D5 checksum algorithm. Because 1 in 10
   random 12-digit numbers accidentally pass Verhoeff, Indian phone numbers starting
   with 91 followed by mobile prefixes [6-9] were being misclassified.
   Algorithm: Enforces `_aadhaar_plausible()` — any 12-digit number starting with `91[6-9]`
   is classified as a telephone number unless explicit Aadhaar formatting is present.

2. Luhn Mod-10 Algorithm (`backend/pii/recognizer.py`):
   Validates credit and debit cards across Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and Amex,
   distinguishing genuine card numbers from random timestamps or serial numbers.

3. Jaro-Winkler String Distance & Token Overlap (`backend/pii/resolver.py`):
   Combines character-level similarity with token matching to evaluate identity
   match confidence without false positive name collisions.

4. k-Anonymity Password Checking (`backend/agent/verifiers.py`):
   Computes SHA-1 of the password. Transmits only the first 5 hexadecimal characters
   to `api.pwnedpasswords.com/range/{prefix}`. Compares the remaining 35 characters
   locally against the returned list of compromised suffixes.

5. Dynamic Privacy Risk Calculator (`backend/pii/risk_calculator.py`):
   Composite 0-100 curve:
     Score = 100 * (1 - e^(-total_weighted_points / scaling_factor))
   Weights: Aadhaar (10.0), Government ID (9.5), Passwords (9.0), PAN (9.0),
   Bank Account (9.0), UPI (6.0), Phone (4.0), Email (3.0).

12. DATA FIDUCIARY DIRECTORY & STATUTORY LEGAL ROUTING
================================================================================

Statutory Legal Routing:
  - DPDP Act 2023 Section 12: Operating Data Fiduciaries must erase personal data
    upon withdrawal of consent or when the original processing purpose is satisfied.
  - DPDP Act 2023 Section 13: Mandatory Grievance Redressal mechanism. Fiduciaries
    must provide accessible redressal before escalation to the DPBI.
  - DPDP Act 2023 Section 27: Prescribes statutory penalties up to ₹250 Crore for
    significant data security and statutory compliance failures.
  - Judicial Records (Indian Kanoon): Section 12 does not apply. Personal data in
    court judgments requires an application to the presiding High Court / Supreme Court.
  - Statutory Registries (MCA Director Filings): Section 3(c)(ii) excludes data
    published under legal duty. Correction applies; statutory erasure does not.
  - Unattributed Dark Web Dumps (Collection #1, Naz.API): No identifiable controller.
    Remedy: Credential rotation and 2FA hardening.
