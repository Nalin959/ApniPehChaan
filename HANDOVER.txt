1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROJECT IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SovereignPrivacy AI is an autonomous, privacy-first personal agent engineered to
empower individuals to reclaim control over their personal digital footprint. In
an ecosystem where sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) is sold
by hundreds of data brokers and exposed in dark-web breaches, the agent bridges
the gap between statutory rights (like India's DPDP Act 2023 Section 12 Right to
Erasure and EU GDPR Article 17) and practical execution.

Key Capabilities:
  1. Multi-Source Threat Intelligence:
     - 1,035+ verified Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) corporate breach incidents.
     - 956 registered commercial data brokers from the Optery directory.
     - 50 synthetic dark-web leak dumps and doxxing paste records.
  2. Algorithmic Indian & Financial PII Detection:
     - Verhoeff checksum algorithm for 12-digit Indian Aadhaar numbers.
     - Luhn mod-10 algorithm for Visa/MasterCard/RuPay financial cards.
     - Strict regex for Indian PAN cards ([A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}), UPI IDs,
       IFSC codes, and Indian phone numbers (+91).
  3. Probabilistic Identity Resolution:
     - Jaro-Winkler string similarity + token overlap to prevent false positives.
  4. 1-Click Statutory Legal Remediation:
     - Auto-generates formal erasure notices citing India DPDP Act 2023 Sec 12/13,
       EU GDPR Article 17, or California CCPA/CPRA § 1798.105.
  5. Cryptographic Proof-of-Dispatch Ledger:
     - Mints immutable SHA-256 digital audit receipts chained block-to-block.
  6. Statutory 30-Day Compliance Tracker:
     - Tracks response clocks (Day 7 reminder, Day 14 warning, Day 30 escalation
       to the Data Protection Board of India - DPBI).
  7. Obsidian Glassmorphism Cyber UI:
     - Real-time animated threat radar, exposure explorer, legal studio,
       compliance kanban, and live PII benchmark lab.

5. KEY ALGORITHMIC INNOVATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Verhoeff Dihedral Group Permutation for Aadhaar
   - Problem: Standard 12-digit regex causes high false-positive rates by matching
     serial numbers, bank accounts, and timestamps.
   - Solution: Implemented the Verhoeff algorithm in PIIRecognizer. Uses multiplication
     (d_table) and permutation (p_table) matrices over dihedral group D5 to check
     the trailing check digit. 100% genuine Aadhaar validation.

B. Luhn Mod-10 Verification for Credit/Debit Cards
   - Validates Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and Amex numbers before flagging as critical
     financial data exposures.

C. Jaro-Winkler Probabilistic Record Linkage
   - Problem: Finding "Aarav Sharma" in a breach dump doesn't necessarily mean it's
     the user.
   - Solution: IdentityResolver computes token overlap and Jaro-Winkler string
     distances across name, email, phone, and city, assigning confidence tiers:
     * Definite Match (>= 0.85)
     * Probable Match (0.65 - 0.84)
     * Possible Match (0.45 - 0.64)

D. Tamper-Evident Chained Cryptographic Ledger
   - Every legal notice generated or dispatched creates an immutable receipt:
       Receipt_N = SHA-256(Receipt_{N-1} | Timestamp | Action | PayloadHash)
   - Guarantees digital proof-of-dispatch for submission to the Data Protection
     Board of India (DPBI) or regulatory courts.

E. Zero-Knowledge Local Architecture
   - Client-side browser hashes user identifiers with SHA-256.
   - Raw Aadhaar/PAN is processed locally on-device and never transmitted to
     untrusted third parties.
