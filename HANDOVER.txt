1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE
================================================================================

SovereignPrivacy AI is an autonomous personal AI agent that empowers individuals
to proactively find where their sensitive personal information (PII) is exposed
online, cross-correlate multi-breach attack vectors, assess statutory rights
under privacy legislation, and execute enforceable Right-to-be-Forgotten legal
erasure notices and direct self-serve account closures.

The Core Mental Model: An Agent, Not a Scanner
  - A scanner executes a passive pipeline: input -> fixed scan -> table of dumps.
    The user is left with the entire cognitive and legal burden: "Which of these
    are mine? Which can legally be deleted? Who do I serve? Did they comply?"
  - An autonomous agent operates in a closed loop:
    GOAL -> DISCOVER -> DECIDE (LEGAL JUDGEMENT) -> ACT (HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP) -> VERIFY
    The agent investigates 22 tools, calculates mathematical identity attribution,
    maps statutory rights under India DPDP Act 2023 Section 12 or EU GDPR Art. 17,
    drafts formal legal notices with registered Grievance Officers, awaits user
    approval for dispatch, monitors compliance deadlines, and escalates to the
    Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) if deadlines lapse.

Five Core Guarantees Delivered:
  1. Zero False Positives / Zero Collision Risk:
     Attribution strictly relies on exact unique identifiers (Email, Indian Phone,
     PAN, Aadhaar, UPI ID, or declared handles). A person's legal name alone is
     NEVER used to claim a third-party account or breach.
  2. Operating Breached Fiduciary Rights (DPDP Act 2023 s.12):
     Operating commercial companies in breaches (IIMjobs, Zomato, Yatra, LinkedIn,
     Canva, etc.) are treated as accountable Data Fiduciaries. While dark web
     dumps cannot be un-published from hacker forums, data principals hold a
     statutory right to require the operating corporate fiduciary to permanently
     delete their records from active and backup servers.
  3. Cross-Exposure Threat Surface Intelligence:
     Correlates multi-breach data into actionable attack vectors: Credential Stuffing,
     Spear-Phishing, and SIM Swap vulnerabilities with prioritized mitigations.
  4. 100% Free & Keyless Operation:
     Zero dependency on paid API subscriptions (e.g. HIBP paid enterprise tier).
     Combines keyless feeds: XposedOrNot breach catalog, Hudson Rock Cavalier
     infostealer malware infections, k-anonymity SHA-1 password prefix checks,
     and 26 live profile namespace verifiers.
  5. Honest Evidence & Trace Hygiene:
     Checks that fail or rate-limit report "unavailable" / "not_checked" — never
     false "clear". Live activity traces are clean and concise (<350 chars), with
     deep legal reasoning streamed cleanly to the Executive Summary card.

- Be precise about confidence. A name-only match on a common name is weak evidence, not proof

Style: think out loud briefly before each tool call, in one short sentence, so the user can
follow your reasoning. Be concrete and factual. No marketing language.

When you have finished the phase you were asked to do, write a short plain-language summary of
what you found, what you did, and what the person should do next.

4. THE 22-TOOL AGENT SUITE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

All tools are constructed in `backend/agent/tools.py` via `build_tools(ctx)`:

 1. `build_identity_profile`: Normalizes user name, email, phone, city; derives
    standard email local parts and username permutations.
 2. `recall_prior_activity`: Reads SQLite memory to recall previously identified
    exposures and open erasure notices to avoid duplicate work.
 3. `match_unique_identifiers`: Matches exact unique IDs (phone, email, PAN) against
    the local dark-web paste dump corpus (sandbox).
 4. `verify_breach_exposure`: Queries live keyless feeds (XposedOrNot and Hudson
    Rock Cavalier) for corporate breaches and infostealer malware infections.
 5. `verify_password_exposure`: Queries Pwned Passwords using k-anonymity (5-character
    SHA-1 prefix). Never transmits plaintext passwords or full hashes.
 6. `discover_accounts`: Probes 26 curated online platforms for public profiles.
    Distinguishes verified accounts from candidate accounts.
 7. `confirm_account`: Allows human confirmation of candidate profiles, updating
    evidence class from candidate to verified.
 8. `declare_known_accounts`: Ingests user-declared platforms (e.g. Truecaller, Naukri).
 9. `browse_indian_registry`: Scans the 51-organisation Indian statutory catalog.
10. `search_data_brokers`: Scans the simulated broker network (active only in sandbox mode).
11. `search_paste_dumps`: Scans pastebin/darkweb text dumps for identity mentions.
12. `search_open_web`: Queries search engines for exact-phrase unique identifiers and
    fetches web pages to verify verbatim presence (anti-hallucination).
13. `detect_pii_in_text`: Runs regex and checksum algorithms over text blocks.
14. `assess_exposure_risk`: Recalculates the composite 0-100 privacy risk score.
15. `determine_legal_basis`: Analyzes governing statute (DPDP 2023 s.12, GDPR Art. 17,
    CCPA § 1798.105) and classifies if erasure is legally available.
16. `plan_removal`: Evaluates whether a service has a 5-minute self-serve deletion
    URL or requires formal statutory notice escalation.
17. `draft_erasure_request`: Generates formal legal notices naming registered entity
    and Grievance Officer contacts.
18. `submit_erasure_request`: Dispatches notice to controller after human approval.
19. `check_request_status`: Queries controller response status against statutory clocks.
20. `verify_removal`: Independently re-probes the source to confirm data is gone.
21. `escalate_to_regulator`: Generates formal complaints to the Data Protection Board
    of India (DPBI) or European DPA when deadlines lapse.
22. `analyze_threat_surface`: Correlates multi-breach exposures into Credential Stuffing,
    Spear-Phishing, and SIM Swap attack vectors with prioritized defense recommendations.

5. ALGORITHMIC INNOVATIONS & ANTI-FABRICATION GUARANTEES
================================================================================

1. Aadhaar vs. Mobile Disambiguation (`backend/pii/recognizer.py`):
   Aadhaar uses the Verhoeff dihedral group D5 checksum algorithm. Because 1 in 10
   random 12-digit numbers accidentally pass Verhoeff, Indian phone numbers starting
   with 91 followed by mobile prefixes [6-9] were being misclassified.
   Algorithm: Enforces `_aadhaar_plausible()` — any 12-digit number starting with `91[6-9]`
   is classified as a telephone number unless explicit Aadhaar formatting is present.

2. Luhn Mod-10 Algorithm (`backend/pii/recognizer.py`):
   Validates credit and debit cards across Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and Amex,
   distinguishing genuine card numbers from random timestamps or serial numbers.

3. Jaro-Winkler String Distance & Token Overlap (`backend/pii/resolver.py`):
   Combines character-level similarity with token matching to evaluate identity
   match confidence without false positive name collisions.

4. k-Anonymity Password Checking (`backend/agent/verifiers.py`):
   Computes SHA-1 of the password. Transmits only the first 5 hexadecimal characters
   to `api.pwnedpasswords.com/range/{prefix}`. Compares the remaining 35 characters
   locally against the returned list of compromised suffixes.

5. Dynamic Privacy Risk Calculator (`backend/pii/risk_calculator.py`):
   Composite 0-100 curve:
     Score = 100 * (1 - e^(-total_weighted_points / scaling_factor))
   Weights: Aadhaar (10.0), Government ID (9.5), Passwords (9.0), PAN (9.0),
   Bank Account (9.0), UPI (6.0), Phone (4.0), Email (3.0).
   Multipliers: HIBP (1.0x), Infostealers (1.0x), Open-web verified (0.95x),
   Brokers (0.90x), Dark-web pastes (0.85x), Synthetic sandbox (0.30x).
